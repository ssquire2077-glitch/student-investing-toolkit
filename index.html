Student Investing Toolkit

A short, practical guide to investing basics, risk and sensible decision-making for UK university students.

Why it looks like this. In a survey of 105 UK students, practical case studies and simulations were the most requested formats, and concentration risk and bond prices were the weakest knowledge areas (fewer than half answered them correctly). The toolkit is built around those findings.

Educational use only. This toolkit is not financial advice. Investments can fall as well as rise, and you can get back less than you put in. Figures in the simulator are illustrative, not predictions.
